Sharing sends things to people you pick. There is no public feed — everything you send has a named
audience, and a post is the thing you send.

## How to use it

1. **Posts** in the sidebar is the conversation: **New** writes one, and you can attach items from
   your library.
2. Choose who sees it. Replies stay with the post.
3. Sharing into Kindredly from a share sheet or a link opens **Add to Kindredly**, which asks where
   it should go — **Save to library** for yourself, **Create post** to send it.
4. **Shared with me** collects what others sent you; **Shared by me** shows what you sent, and who
   got it.

## Notes

Sharing a collection shares that collection, not your library.

Sending to several groups at once normally makes one post everyone can see. An admin can switch on
**Separate recipients** instead, which gives each group its own private thread.

Posts need a server, so they're unavailable in Device Only Mode.
