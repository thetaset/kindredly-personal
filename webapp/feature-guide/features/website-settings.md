Website settings takes the pulling parts out of big sites — recommendations, endless feeds, comment
threads — and leaves the rest working.

## How to use it

1. Open **Family**, pick the person, then **Website Settings**.
2. Turn off the parts of each site you don't want them pulled into.

**YouTube** and **Reddit** each have switches for search, comments, and the browse surfaces that
keep you scrolling. **Search** can show matching items from your own library beside your results on
Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo.

## Notes

Admin only — a parent or guardian sets this, for a child or for themselves.

For a restricted child every distraction switch starts on. For everyone else they start off, so
nothing changes until you ask for it.

The video still plays and the page still works — this hides surfaces, it doesn't block the site. Use
**Content filtering** or **Library Mode** for that.
