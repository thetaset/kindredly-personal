The Kindredly Router covers the whole house. Filtering runs on the network itself, so it reaches
the devices that can't run an extension — a games console, a smart TV, a visiting friend's tablet.

## Notes

Coming soon, and the earliest of the three — it's hardware, and it's still being built.

It's a complement to the apps rather than a replacement. A router sees traffic but not what's on
screen, so page-level filtering and app limits still come from
[Kindredly Guard](/features/kindredly-guard) and
[Kindredly Companion](/features/kindredly-companion).
