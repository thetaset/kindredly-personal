A subscription follows a feed — a blog, a podcast, or a published Kindredly collection — and
gathers what's new in one place, inside your library rather than out on the web.

## How to use it

1. Open **Library → Subscriptions**.
2. **Add subscription** searches your library for feeds, or finds published ones.
3. Pick how you want to read it: **What's New** puts the latest first, **Work-Through** hands you
   one at a time at the pace you set — your own, daily, every few days, or weekly.
4. Group related subscriptions so the feed can be filtered to one thing at a time.

## Notes

Manage the whole list, including custom names and pacing, under **Settings → Subscriptions**.

A parent can subscribe a child when adding a published feed to their library.

Needs a server to pull from, so it isn't available in Device Only Mode.
