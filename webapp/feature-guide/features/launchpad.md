Launchpad is the row of apps and shortcuts on Home, plus the full-screen overlay behind it. The
overlay also holds your open browser tabs and app-like items from your library.

## How to use it

1. The strip sits on **Home**, under the search bar.
2. Tap the grid icon for the full overlay.
3. Drag a tile to reorder it. The order syncs across your devices.
4. To add something, save it to your library and tag it with the kind of app it is.

## Settings

**Settings → Default apps** sets which app handles search, mail, notes and music, and whether the
strip shows on Home.

Turning the strip off keeps the overlay — the grid icon still opens it.
