Tasks are lists of things to do — chores, homework, a morning routine — kept per person. What's due
today appears on the Today Dashboard.

The Calendar is the same tasks laid out by month, so you can see the week ahead rather than just
what is due now. It has no switch of its own: turning Tasks on brings both, and turning it off takes
both away. A calendar with no tasks in it would be an empty grid.
