Usage limits are daily time budgets that account for what the time was spent on. Content type and
activity type are tracked separately, so a video limit applies to video regardless of its value, and
a low-value-content limit applies regardless of format.

## How to use it

1. Open **Settings → Usage limits**.
2. Set an overall daily budget. Most families need nothing else.
3. Add category budgets only where the overall one isn't enough.
4. Use schedules to make a limit differ by day.

## Notes

For a child, open the same panel from their page under **Family**. Limits belong to the person, so
they apply across phone, laptop and tablet.
