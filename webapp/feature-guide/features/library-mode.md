Library Mode limits a child to the items in their library. Everything else is blocked, so what they
can reach is exactly what someone put there.

## How to use it

1. Open **Family**, pick the child, then **Content blocking & filtering**.
2. Turn on **Library Access Only**.
3. Save things to their library — anything you add becomes reachable.

Suggested for kids 13 and under.

Content filtering stays on underneath it. Library Mode decides what's reachable; filtering still
checks what comes back.

## Letting them explore

**Explore Kindredly catalog** sits directly under Library Access Only and is off until you turn it
on. It gives the child an **Explore** tab where they can browse curated collections and tap **Ask to
add** on anything they want. The request lands in your access requests, and approving it adds the
item to their library. They choose what to ask for; you decide what arrives.

It's per-child and admin-only, and the switch only appears once that child is in Library Mode.
Adding a catalog item to a library always goes through the request, on the server — a child can't
add one to themselves.
