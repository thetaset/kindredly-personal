Page Monitor puts a small readout on the page showing what Kindredly is doing there — content
filtering, image scanning, ad detection.

Useful when you want to know why a page was blocked or an image was blurred, instead of guessing.

## How to use it

1. Turn it on with the switch above.
2. Browse to any page. The widget sits in a corner.
3. Tap it to expand the detail for that page.

## Notes

Experimental, and personal to you.

It reports what already happens — turning it off doesn't change any filtering or scanning, only
whether you can see it.
