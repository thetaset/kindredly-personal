The Today Dashboard gathers what needs you today — saved reading, tasks, practice, and time spent —
onto one screen.

## How to use it

1. Open **Today** from the sidebar, or the Today tab on a phone.
2. Each card is one kind of thing waiting on you.
3. Tap an item to open it, or the card heading for the full list.
4. Cards with nothing waiting hide themselves.

## Settings

**Settings → Today** turns individual cards on and off.

The usage card stays visible even when nothing else does, as long as activity tracking is on.
