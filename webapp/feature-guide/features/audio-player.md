Kindredly plays audio itself, so a podcast episode or a saved recording opens here rather than
handing you off to another app.

## How it works

Start anything audio in your library and a small player appears at the bottom of the screen. It
stays there as you move around, so you can keep listening while you read or search. Open it for the
full player, the queue, and playback speed.

Episodes you download work offline and are kept for a week by default. Change how long, and manage
what's stored, under **Settings → Downloads**.

## Where to find it

The **Audio** tile in the Launchpad opens the player, and the mini bar appears on its own whenever
something is playing.

The tile stays out of the way until there is something to hear — no track, nothing queued and
nothing part-listened means no tile.

## Turning it off

The switch above hides the Audio tile and the mini player bar. Audio in your library still opens
normally; you just don't get the persistent player.

You can also set whether the tile sits on the Launchpad strip under
**Settings → Default Apps**.
