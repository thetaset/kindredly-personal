Your library is everything you've saved — pages, notes, videos, files and collections. It is private
to you, and it's what the rest of Kindredly works from.

## How to use it

1. Save something with the browser extension, from a share sheet, or with **Add to Kindredly**.
2. **New** shows what arrived most recently.
3. **Read Later** holds what you meant to get to; **Rediscover** brings back older saves.
4. **Collections** group items into sets — a reading list, a course, links for one child — and
   **New collection** starts you from a template or a blank one.
5. Search covers the whole library, and works offline.

## Notes

Items are encrypted on your device before they leave it, so what you saved isn't readable on the
server.

A child in Library Mode can reach what's in their library and nothing else.

How much you can keep depends on the plan: Standard holds 2,000 items and 500 MB of files, Plus
holds 50,000 and 10 GB.

Building a collection with AI is a Plus feature, on the webapp, for parents. Making one by hand is
included.
